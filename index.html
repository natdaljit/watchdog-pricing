Calculation basis (matches Excel Watchdog calc)
  • Concentric annular air reservoir (tank tube around cylinder tube). Volume uses tank ID, cylinder OD (= bore + 2×wall), and total length = stroke + extra.
  • Isothermal expansion (Boyle): Pfinal = Pinit × Vtank / (Vtank + Vcyl). Fail-close force = Pfinal × piston area.
  • Pinit = line pressure (no booster) or 2× line pressure (with 2:1 amplifier).
  • Only standard VA bore/tank sizes. Cylinder must also satisfy Operating Force (area × line pressure) ≥ Required Force.
  • FSF = 1.00. Cost = tank base (0.7 × VA cyl base) + total stroke × tank $/in (0.3 × VA) + $1000 Watchdog (no booster) or + $1500 booster.
  • Rod diameter taken from tank size data; female rod thread from acting cylinder bore data.
